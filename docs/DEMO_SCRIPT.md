# PF X-RAY — 2-Minute Video Demo Script & Screen Recording Runbook

**Target Length:** 120 Seconds (2:00)  
**Tone:** Confident, empathetic, crisp product demonstration.  
**Presenter:** Screen recording + voiceover.

---

## ⏱️ Video Timeline & Word-for-Word Script

### 0:00 – 0:20 | The Problem (Hook)
- **Visual:** Open on the Landing Page (`/`). Scroll smoothly over the headline: *"1 in 5 PF claims gets rejected. Catch yours before you file."*
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Every month, millions of Indian workers try to withdraw or transfer their hard-earned Provident Fund. But over 20% of claims get rejected.
  > The reason? Tiny, fixable mismatches like a missing Date of Exit, an unverified bank account, or a spelling difference between UAN and Aadhaar.
  > Worst of all, EPFO just gives you a cryptic rejection code and leaves you stranded.
  > This is **PF X-RAY**."

---

### 0:20 – 0:45 | Pre-Flight & Intake (The 30-Second Check)
- **Visual:** Click **"Run a pre-flight check"** -> Lands on `/intake?tab=samples`. Click on sample case **RC04 (Missing Date of Exit)**.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "With PF X-Ray, you don't need to understand government jargon. You just tell us what you're trying to do.
  > You can paste your exact rejection remark, or test a pre-flight check before submitting.
  > Let's take a common case: an employee who switched jobs, but their transfer failed."

---

### 0:45 – 1:15 | The Money Shot: Claim X-Ray & Case Reconstruction
- **Visual:** Click **"Run Pre-Flight Check"** -> Lands on `/diagnosis`. Highlight the **Claim X-Ray** box. Point mouse to the work-history timeline showing the previous employer marked with `⚠ Date of Exit: not updated` and the comparison table.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Here is our signature innovation: **Claim X-Ray**.
  > Instead of a generic error message, the system reconstructs the member's complete case.
  > It immediately pins the exact blocker: *Your previous employer has not marked your Date of Exit.*
  > Look at the work history timeline: previous company Northwind Systems is flagged with the exact missing exit date, while the current employer is active.
  > It shows what records were compared, gives a *Confirmed by records* confidence score, and tells you exactly who must act."

---

### 1:15 – 1:45 | The Resolution Plan & 1-Click Action
- **Visual:** Click **"Build Resolution Plan: Employer Date-of-Exit request"** -> Lands on `/action`. Show the pre-generated formal **Employer Request Letter**, click **"Copy"**, show the **Print/Download** and **Share via WhatsApp** buttons.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Understanding the problem is only half the battle. PF X-Ray gives the citizen the complete remedy.
  > Here is the **Resolution Plan**.
  > It outlines the exact 3-step fix, lists required documents, and generates a pre-formatted, legally accurate letter ready to send to the employer's HR or payroll team.
  > One click to copy, print as PDF, or share directly over WhatsApp."

---

### 1:45 – 2:00 | Language Toggle & Closing Impact
- **Visual:** Click the language toggle to switch to **हिन्दी**. Navigate to `/tracker` to show the 4-stage lifecycle tracker. End on the clean home screen.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Everything is fully bilingual in English and Hindi, accessible to WCAG 2.2 standards, and built on privacy-first synthetic data.
  > We're not just showing citizens what failed. We're showing them how to move forward.
  > That is PF X-RAY. Thank you."

---

## 🎬 Quick Recording Checklist
1. **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps / 30fps.
2. **Browser:** Chrome/Brave in clean window (no bookmark bar or distracting tabs).
3. **URL:** `http://localhost:3000` (or deployed Vercel URL).
4. **Mic:** Clear audio with no background noise.
