# PF X-RAY — Hackathon Submission

## 1. Project Title
**PF X-RAY (EPFO Claim-Rejection Prevention & Case Diagnosis Intelligence)**

---

## 2. 250-Word Official Submission Summary

Over 1 in 5 EPFO claims in India gets rejected—not because citizens aren't eligible, but due to minor, fixable data mismatches like unapproved bank KYC, missing dates of exit, or name spelling discrepancies across UAN and Aadhaar. When a claim fails, citizens receive cryptic rejection remarks with zero guidance, leading to repeated rejections, lost retirement savings, and endless portal frustration.

**PF X-RAY** reverses the government paradigm from bureaucratic complexity to citizen intent. Instead of forcing users to navigate scattered government menus, PF X-RAY provides an **intent-first operating experience** powered by a deterministic rules engine with LLM-assisted plain-language explanations. 

Our signature innovation, **Claim X-Ray**, performs instant case reconstruction: it aligns work-history timelines, compares underlying records (UAN, Aadhaar, PAN, Bank IFSC, Date of Exit), pins the single blocker, and assigns an honest confidence tier (Confirmed / Likely / Needs Verification). It then generates a **1-Click Resolution Packet** containing a step-by-step checklist, an editable employer request letter, a Joint Declaration draft, and WhatsApp sharing formats.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and WCAG 2.2 AA compliant bilingual UI (English & हिन्दी), PF X-RAY operates with 100% synthetic demo records and zero real PII exposure. It transforms uncertainty into confidence, giving millions of Indian workers the exact answers they need to resolve their PF claims in under 60 seconds.

---

## 3. Key Differentiators & Highlights
- **Deterministic Rules Core + AI Explanation**: Mission-critical diagnosis uses rigid rule taxonomy with LLM fallback for conversational clarity.
- **Claim X-Ray**: Instant case autopsy and visual work-history reconciliation.
- **Actionable Output**: Auto-generated pre-formatted employer letters and Joint Declaration forms.
- **Bilingual & Accessible**: Native English and Hindi support with high contrast and zero color-only status indicators.
- **Privacy-First**: Operates strictly on client-side synthetic cases; no real citizen PII ingested.
